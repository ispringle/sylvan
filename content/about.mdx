# Howdy, I'm Ian.

![A new-born Elsa and I, sitting on a couch enjoying a beer.](./elsa-and-i.jpeg)

I'm a sinner saved by grace. I am a husband and father.

I work as a software developer by day, but after hours I try to keep technology
in its place. I am something of a neo-Luddite, and I work hard to balance modern
tools with a desire to work with my hands.

My interests are many and varied. I have a degree in New Testament studies, with
a minor in Koine Greek, and I love studying and discussing theology. I also live
on a farm in very rural Missouri with my wife and children, so I spend a lot of
time being a cowboy, dairy farmer, vet, larborer, general handy man, and whatever
else the day might require.

I am also drawn to the Middle Ages: their literature above all, but also the
languages that carried it, Latin and English, and the wider imaginative
world those texts inhabit: the theology that ordered it, the cosmologies that
mapped heaven and earth, and the myths and legends that still cling to the
margins of the page. I am currently pursuing an MA in Medieval Literature
and Language, so while I don't have any standing at all, it won't prevent
me from pretending I do!
