Alcohol (ethyl alchohl technically) is the least desired form of caloric energy
for your body, although your body does actually require alcohol to live, but it
gets all it needs internally.  In fact alcohol and fiber are the only energies
the body will throw out. It's hard to digest of all.

Acetyle-CoA is super important to the "kreb cycle"
which is the means by which the body extracts energy from food, but again the
body makes alcohol naturally plus consumes it in all unprocessed fruits and
vegetables, so there isn't a need for additional intake to live (unless your an
alcoholic, which is actually a physiological change to how your body
digests _all_ food and it basically leans heavily on Acetyle-CoA).
