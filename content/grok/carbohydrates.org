#+title: carbohydrates
#+date: <2023-08-25 Fri 08:53>
#+filetags:   :diet:macronutrients:


Carbohydrates are the easiest form of calorie to convert (except things that
require fermentation like grasses and other "roughage", we do not have the means
of fermenting food the way ruminants, horses, etc. do). Some carbs are easier
than others but they're all (starch, sugar, fiber) converted in the exact same
way and result in glucose

(food -> dextrin/maltose -> lactase/sucrase/maltase -> monosaccharides ->
glycogen -> glucose)

This form of energy is not long lasting, your body can only store glucose in one
way, fat. All the energy goes into your blood stream and whatever isn't consumed
is then converted into fat reserves.
