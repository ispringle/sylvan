#+title: emacs
#+date: <2022-08-04 Thu>

I have an off and on relationship with emacs. I'll use emacs for a few months at
a time, once I even got a full year's worth in, and then something will annoy me
and I'll go back to using neovim and a terminal for a while, but then I'll have
a craving for org-mode or something and swap back to emacs. I really would love
to just settle down, I think I'd pick emacs if it there wasn't something in the
back of my mind telling me that emacs is bloatware... nonetheless I still love
it. 

I formerly used Doom, however my curiosity got the best of me and I ended up
writing my own [[file:../emacs.org][emacs config]] and have been using that with stock emacs
keybindings ever since.
