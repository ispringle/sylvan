#+title: A Short Canoe Trip
#+filetags: :life:

Went canoeing down the Eleven Point River last weekend. The plan was to camp out
on the river over night, but we ended up not going far enough up river to make a
two-day trip very viable, so we ended up finishing the 14 mile trip in one day.
I'm definitely planning on going back again and starting at the starting point
of the river up near Tucker, MO.
